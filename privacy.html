Vilusic Player
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

In short: Vilusic Player works entirely on your PC. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data, and it does not connect to the internet.

Who we are

Vilusic Player is a music player for Windows published by JoshiVilas (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or telemetry, and it makes no network requests.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app keeps a small amount of data locally on your PC. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your music folder. The folder you choose is remembered so your library loads next time. Windows grants the app access only to that folder.
  • Settings. Your theme, interface style, volume, mute, shuffle and repeat choices.

The app reads song details (such as title, artist, album and duration) from the audio files in the folder you choose, only to display and play them. Your music files are never modified, uploaded or shared.

Windows media controls

While a song plays, the app shows its title and artist in the Windows media controls (for example, the volume pop-up and lock screen) so you can control playback. This information is handled by Windows on your device and is not sent to us.

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any data, we have nothing to share, sell or disclose to third parties.

Deleting your data

You can clear the remembered music folder at any time with the Forget folder button in the app. Uninstalling Vilusic Player removes all of its settings from your PC.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Microsoft Store

The app is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect information about downloads and purchases under its own privacy statement, which we do not control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
